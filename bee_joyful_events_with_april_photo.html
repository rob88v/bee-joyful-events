Meet the Planner

Planning with care, creativity, and calm confidence.

Hi, I’m April — creator and lead planner of Bee Joyful Events. For more than 10 years, I’ve had the joy of helping people bring their event visions to life.

My planning style is rooted in communication, efficiency, creativity, and genuine care. Whether you are planning an intimate celebration, milestone event, or once-in-a-lifetime wedding, my goal is to make the entire process feel organized, personal, and joyful.

Born and raised in Fort Lauderdale, Florida, I have also spent over 16 years working in education — a background that strengthened my ability to listen deeply, manage complex moving parts, solve unexpected problems, and guide people with patience.

Outside of planning events, I’m happily married to the love of my life, sharing our home with our two dogs, Winston and Basil. My family, friends, and two nephews are a huge part of my world — and they inspire the heart behind everything we do.
